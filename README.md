## What I am building now

[bishalregmi.com](https://bishalregmi.com) runs a [World Cup 2026 prediction
engine](https://bishalregmi.com/world-cup) that re-simulates the tournament two
million times a day and sharpens its forecasts as real results lock in. The full
stack, Angular to FastAPI to Postgres, runs on my own hardware and updates itself
daily.

## How I work

I care more about point-in-time correctness, honest baselines, and walk-forward
validation than about a flashy accuracy number. Reproducible pipelines, typed APIs,
and small reviewable commits. I would rather report a true null result than ship a
leaky model that only looks good.
